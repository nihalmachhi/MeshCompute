Split big work. Survive failure. Merge the answer.

MeshCompute breaks a job into durable tasks, leases them to workers, recovers work when a worker disappears, and merges the results, with schedulers you can compare on the same workload.

FAQ

What does at-least-once mean here?

Tasks may run more than once after a worker disappears; handlers must be idempotent.

What is a fencing token?

The attempt number prevents stale workers from committing results.

What happens when a worker dies mid-task?

The lease expires and the reaper requeues the task.

Why PostgreSQL as the queue?

SKIP LOCKED gives durable, transactional claiming.

How do schedulers differ?

FIFO, fair share, and shortest-job-first have different wait/fairness tradeoffs.

What is speculative execution?

Duplicate slow tasks; first committed result wins.

How are images handled safely?

Allow-listed handlers, path confinement, pixel limits.

How is duplicate detection evaluated?

Precision/recall on generated transformed images.

Is it production ready?

It is a compact reference system, not a managed queue.
